# ✨ Medea Beauty Lounge

Modern website and digital management experience developed for **Medea Beauty Lounge**, a beauty and wellness business in Italy.

The project was designed and developed as a real client solution, combining a premium public-facing website with booking flows, service presentation, gallery management and administrative features.

## 🌐 About the Project

Medea Beauty Lounge was created to provide the business with a modern digital presence that reflects its brand identity while making it easier for customers to discover treatments, learn about the team and request appointments.

The platform combines customer-facing content with administrative functionality for managing parts of the website without modifying the source code.

- Product management
- Authentication
